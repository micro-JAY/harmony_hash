## Why

Tonari's three public product policies should use one recognizable, accessible modal presentation and one verified operator/contact contract. Harmony Hash already supplies the target compact policy layout, but its heading casing and regression contract need to be aligned with the shared release.

## What Changes

- Display the English policy heading as `PRIVACY POLICY` while retaining the existing localized footer trigger, Japanese heading, focus lifecycle, and compact single-column modal.
- Preserve the legally important individual-operator identity, private-address-on-request wording, and `privacy@tonari.ai` contact in both languages.
- Confirm that the policy contains no raw analytics property identifier and keep its provider disclosures source-faithful to the current Harmony runtime.
- Extend focused unit and browser coverage so the shared modal geometry, casing, operator identity, contact address, scrolling, and mobile containment remain stable.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `app-shell`: The footer policy action and responsive policy dialog gain a shared cross-product presentation and verified public-copy contract.

## Impact

The change is limited to Harmony's privacy copy, policy modal regression coverage, and the canonical app-shell requirement. It does not alter Hanz, progression generation, providers, Worker routes, production configuration, or the merged OpenAI migration behavior.
